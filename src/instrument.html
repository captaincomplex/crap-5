The slide edges carry mm, inches and nautical miles at 1:250,000 and 1:500,000. Screen magnification is not a physical-size guarantee. The ruler labels describe the virtual slide; use the chart-length conversion below for measured paper-chart distances. Do not treat screen pixels as physical millimetres.

Operation

Auto mode: click the wind window to pencil a cross, drag inside it to slide the grid, or drag the compass ring to rotate. The named buttons lock a single action. On the wind side, align the wind direction at the top index, then pencil a cross below the centre dot by the wind speed. Set TAS at the centre dot and rotate to heading. Read groundspeed and drift beneath the cross. Crosses and pencil lines stay attached to the transparent disc. Changing L/H clears marks because the velocity scale changes. Use Pencil line for drift-line intersections at a turning point. On the calculation side, set ratios with the inner disc and read both scales under the cursor; determine decimal position separately.
